Our Services

A wide range of health care services under one roof, available 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Departments

Complete care, one campus

YAHSHUA MEDICAL CLINICS LTD prides a wide range of health care services, delivered by qualified doctors, nurses and technicians.

🩺

General Medicine & X-Ray

Complete adult consultation, diagnosis and treatment by our General Practitioners, backed by on site general X-Ray imaging.

  • Adult outpatient consultations
  • Chronic disease follow up
  • General X-Ray imaging
  • Medical check ups & certificates
💉

Minor Surgery

Safe, sterile minor surgical procedures performed by experienced clinicians in our dedicated procedure room.

  • Wound care, suturing & dressing
  • Abscess incision & drainage
  • Excision of small lumps
  • Postoperative follow up
🏥

All OPD Services

A full outpatient department covering triage, consultation, cashier, pharmacy and treatment, organised for a short, clear patient journey.

  • Reception & triage
  • Consultation rooms
  • Injection & dressing room
  • Nursing service
🩸

Gynecology & Obstetrics OPD

Dedicated women's health care, from routine gynecological consultation to antenatal follow up.

  • Gynecological consultations
  • Antenatal & postnatal care
  • Reproductive health advice
  • Ultrasound referral support
🦷

Dental Services & OPG X-Ray

A complete dental unit led by a Dental Surgeon and Dental Therapist, with digital OPG (panoramic) X-Ray on site.

  • OPG panoramic & dental X-Ray
  • Scaling & professional cleaning
  • Fillings & restorations
  • Tooth extraction & minor oral surgery
  • Root canal treatment
  • Dentures & prosthetics
  • Braces / orthodontic advice
  • Teeth whitening & cosmetic dentistry
🔬

Laboratory

A well equipped laboratory delivering accurate, timely results so treatment can start without delay, available day and night.

  • Haematology & biochemistry
  • Malaria, HIV & rapid tests
  • Urine, stool & microbiology
  • Pregnancy & hormonal tests
👶

Pediatrics OPD

Gentle, child focused care for newborns, infants and children, with growth monitoring and nutrition guidance.

  • Child consultations
  • Growth & nutrition monitoring
  • Treatment of childhood illness
  • Parent counselling
🏥

Physiotherapy

Hands on rehabilitation to restore movement, relieve pain and rebuild strength after injury, surgery, stroke or long illness.

  • Musculoskeletal & back pain therapy
  • Postoperative & fracture rehabilitation
  • Stroke & neurological rehabilitation
  • Sports injury management
  • Joint mobilisation & therapeutic exercise
  • Pediatric & geriatric physiotherapy
👩‍👩‍👦

Family Planning

Confidential family planning counselling and a range of modern methods, offered with respect and privacy.

  • Counselling & method choice
  • Short and long term methods
  • Follow up & management
  • Couple counselling
💉

Vaccination

Routine childhood immunisation and adult vaccination, administered by trained nurses following national guidelines.

  • Childhood immunisation
  • Adult & travel vaccines
  • Tetanus prophylaxis
  • Vaccination records
🛋

Hospitalization

Comfortable observation and admission beds with 24 hour nursing care and doctor review for patients who need to stay.

  • 24 hour nursing care
  • Observation & admission beds
  • IV therapy & monitoring
  • Daily doctor review
