Our Services

A wide range of health care services under one roof, available 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Departments

Complete care in one clinic

YAHSHUA MEDICAL CLINICS LTD prides a wide range of health care services, delivered by qualified doctors, nurses, lab technicians and paramedicals.

🩺

General Medicine

Complete adult consultation, diagnosis and treatment by our qualified General Practitioners, covering both acute illnesses and chronic health needs.

  • Adult outpatient consultations
  • Clinical diagnosis & medical treatment
  • General health check ups
  • Medical certificates & fitness exams
🏥

All OPD Services

A structured outpatient department offering fast vital signs triage, routine check ups, consultations, injections, dressings and nursing care.

  • General check ups & routine screening
  • Vital signs triage & priority care
  • Doctor consultation rooms
  • Injection & sterile dressing room
  • Outpatient nursing care
👶

Pediatrics OPD

Gentle, child focused care for newborns, infants, children and adolescents, with growth monitoring and nutrition guidance.

  • Childhood illness diagnosis & care
  • Growth & nutrition monitoring
  • Newborn & infant health checks
  • Parent counselling & guidance
💉

Minor Surgery

All sterile minor surgical procedures performed by experienced clinicians in our dedicated procedure room.

  • Circumcision (safe clinical circumcision)
  • Wound care, suturing & sterile dressing
  • Abscess incision & drainage
  • Excision of small lumps & cysts
  • Postoperative follow up
🔬

Laboratory

A well equipped diagnostic laboratory delivering accurate, timely results so treatment can start without delay, available day and night.

  • Haematology & biochemistry tests
  • Malaria, HIV & rapid diagnostic tests
  • Urinalysis, stool exam & microbiology
  • Pregnancy & hormonal tests
  • Blood glucose & lipid profile
👩‍👩‍👦

Family Planning

Confidential reproductive health counselling and a full range of modern family planning methods, offered with respect and privacy.

  • Confidential counselling & method choice
  • Short term & long term modern methods
  • Implant & injectable methods
  • Couple counselling & follow up
💉

Vaccination

Routine childhood immunisation and adult vaccination administered by trained nurses following national health guidelines.

  • Routine childhood immunisation (EPI)
  • Adult & travel vaccines
  • Tetanus prophylaxis
  • Official vaccination records & cards
🩸

Gynecology & Obstetrics OPD

Dedicated women's health care, from routine gynecological consultations to antenatal and postnatal maternal care.

  • Gynecological consultations & exams
  • Antenatal & postnatal maternal care
  • Reproductive health screening
  • Ultrasound referral support
🩺

Non-Communicable Diseases (NCDs)

Dedicated screening, treatment and continuous long term management for chronic non-communicable diseases to protect your health.

  • Hypertension (high blood pressure) management
  • Diabetes screening & glucose monitoring
  • Asthma & chronic respiratory care
  • Cardiovascular risk assessment & follow up
  • Dietary, exercise & lifestyle counselling
📸

Medical Imaging & X-Ray

Digital diagnostic general X-Ray imaging on site for fast, accurate skeletal, chest and trauma radiography with rapid reporting.

  • General diagnostic X-Ray imaging
  • Chest radiography for lungs & heart
  • Bone, spine & joint fracture imaging
  • Fast digital radiography reporting
🛋

Hospitalization

Comfortable observation and inpatient admission beds with 24 hour nursing care and daily doctor review for patients who need to stay.

  • 24 hour continuous nursing care
  • Comfortable observation & admission beds
  • Intravenous (IV) therapy & monitoring
  • Daily doctor ward rounds & review
🦷

Dental Services & OPG X-Ray

A complete dental clinic led by our Dental Surgeon, with digital OPG panoramic dental X-Ray on site, saving Eastern Province patients from travelling to Kigali.

  • OPG panoramic & dental X-Ray
  • Scaling & professional cleaning
  • Tooth coloured fillings & restorations
  • Root canal treatment
  • Tooth extraction & minor oral surgery
  • Dentures, crowns & bridges
  • Braces / orthodontic advice
  • Teeth whitening & cosmetic dentistry
🏃

Physiotherapy

Hands on rehabilitation to restore movement, relieve pain and rebuild strength after injury, surgery, stroke or long illness.

  • Musculoskeletal & back pain therapy
  • Postoperative & fracture rehabilitation
  • Stroke & neurological rehabilitation
  • Sports injury management
  • Joint mobilisation & therapeutic exercise
  • Pediatric & geriatric physiotherapy
Dental unit

Dental Services & OPG X-Ray

Imaging

OPG Panoramic X-Ray

Full mouth panoramic digital imaging for accurate diagnosis, extractions, implants and orthodontic planning, right here in Nyagatare.

Preventive

Scaling & Cleaning

Professional scaling, polishing and gum care to prevent decay and periodontal disease.

Restorative

Fillings & Root Canal

Tooth coloured fillings, restorations and root canal treatment to save damaged teeth.

Surgical

Extraction & Oral Surgery

Simple and surgical extractions, including wisdom teeth, performed under local anaesthesia.

Prosthetic

Dentures & Crowns

Partial and complete dentures, crowns and bridges to restore your smile and chewing.

Orthodontic

Braces & Alignment

Assessment and management for crooked or crowded teeth, with orthodontic guidance.

Cosmetic

Whitening & Aesthetics

Teeth whitening and cosmetic finishing for a brighter, confident smile.

Children

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle dental care, fluoride application and oral health education for children.
