HOW TO PUBLISH THE OPENING CEREMONY VIDEO
==========================================
1. Name your video file exactly:  opening-ceremony.mp4
2. Put it in this folder:  public/videos/opening-ceremony.mp4
3. Commit & push to GitHub (or redeploy on Vercel). The video will then
   play automatically on the Home page in the "Watch Our Story" section.
Tip: keep the file under ~40 MB (720p MP4) for fast loading.
